# Items, equipment and props

Use I1 for compact item construction and F2 for thickness/form in [sources](sources.md). The material and contact prompts are practical synthesis; defer to a game's specific restrictions.

## Draw function before ornament

Establish the footprint and viewing angle. Block the defining silhouette: blade and handle, bottle and stopper, book and spine, key and teeth. Reserve room for thickness and the negative spaces that distinguish the object. Check recognition in its inventory slot before engraving, scratches or trim.

Divide the object into meaningful material masses. Choose value differences that explain orientation and construction. Metal, leather, wood and glass should not all receive the same scattered sparkle texture. Use highlights only when permitted by the brief and useful at display size.

Keep functional joins convincing: a blade attaches to a hilt; a strap connects to its anchors; a shield has coherent thickness. When the object is held, review it with the hand rather than trusting an isolated item preview. Inventory art and held equipment can have different projection and scale requirements.

## Work as a family

Compare items together for view angle, edge treatment, material shading and perceived weight. Distinguish important variants through shape or useful details, rather than color alone. A rarity treatment should not erase an object's identity.

For pickup animation, choose an understandable, restrained system. I1 discusses different idle behaviors and collection feedback; apply them according to game function rather than decorating every object with every effect.

## Diagnose and revise

| Failure | Correction |
| --- | --- |
| Weapon looks like a hairline | Revisit width, plane changes and functional parts |
| All materials look glittery | Remove incidental dots; establish broader material shading |
| Item is muddy at slot size | Reduce competing details and strengthen the defining mass |
| Larger preview reveals no extra information | Reuse the base image if sufficient; do not invent redundant variants |
| Artwork crosses an empty inventory-mask cell | Repair the silhouette/placement in the editable source |

Check source size and actual inventory/reward views. Respect mask, canvas and padding rules supplied by the game; these are distinct from character weapon proportions.
